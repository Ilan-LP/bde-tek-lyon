### Usage guidelines
- Primary blue (#145da0): primary actions, headers, key UI elements.
- Deep navy (#050a30): dark backgrounds, high-contrast text areas.
- Sky blue (#b9d1f9): light backgrounds, cards, subtle highlights.
- Off-white (#e8e8e8): replaces pure white in all contexts.
- Accents (yellow, green, pink, beige): used sparingly for highlights, tags, badges, decorative elements.
- Gray (#8a8d91): secondary text, placeholders, disabled states.

## Typography

- **Display / Hero text**: Gagalin - large titles and prominent headings.
- **Main UI / Body**: Bebas Neue - labels, section titles, buttons, general text.

## Poster & Print Layout Style

### Background & composition
- Soft radial gradient (Sky Blue → Off-White), never a flat/solid fill.
- 2-3 blurred pastel "blob" shapes (ellipses, Gaussian blur ~60-70px) placed in corners, using Sky Blue or white, to create a soft "wave" atmosphere. Never geometric or sharp-edged shapes.
- Generous margins and breathing room; avoid cramped layouts.
- Limit each visual to 2-3 colors from the palette at once, never all 9 simultaneously.
- No hard flat color blocks: everything is gradiented or blurred to keep the light, pastel, aquatic feel.

### Logo treatment
- On light/pastel backgrounds, recolor the logo to Main Blue (#145da0) rather than white, for contrast.
- Add a soft drop shadow (blur ~16px, slight downward offset, ~55% opacity, Deep Navy) for subtle depth.
- The jellyfish-only silhouette (no wordmark) can be used as a large, low-opacity watermark (~35% opacity, Sky Blue) tucked in a corner to fill empty space, kept outside the primary content area.

### Typography usage
- Titles/headers: Gagalin, matches the logo's rough brush-stroke style.
- Body/labels/other text: Bebas Neue, with added letter-spacing (tracking) for a cleaner, airier look than the font's default tight spacing.

### Graphic elements
- Functional shapes (icons, stamp circles, form elements, etc.): simple outline in Main Blue (~4px stroke), white or transparent fill, no heavy color fills. Keep decorative elements understated, never bold or saturated.
